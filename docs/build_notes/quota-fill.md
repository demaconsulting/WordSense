# Quota filling

This note records the filling of the per-sense quotas of the encoder corpus (the plan is in `corpus/coverage/README.md`)
and the replacement of the placeholder glosses of project senses. The scripts are in `tools/quota-fill` (see its
README); the work files (sentence pool, batches and answers) are in the git-ignored `tools/real-text/cache/quota/`.
Every figure of the retraining is measured on the balanced, targeted encoder corpus: **it is not the accuracy on
representative text** (see "Targeted rows" below).

## Placeholder glosses of project senses

A project sense (a sense that Open English WordNet does not have; source `dema`, synset `dema-syn-...`) first had a
placeholder gloss. The detection rule (`tools/quota-fill/placeholder_glosses.py detect`): the sense is a project sense
and its gloss is empty, a bare word or hyphenated pair of words (`noun`, `verb`, `adj-noun`), the seeder template ("The
preposition "about"; a project sense, because Open English WordNet has no preposition sense of this word.") or ends with
"no definition recorded". The run found **937** such glosses (817 template, 118 bare word, 2 "no definition recorded");
they were replaced by one-line definitions written by `gpt-6.1-sol` from the lemma, the part of speech, the role of the
sense (a closed class, an interjection), the other senses of the lemma and one or two example sentences of the corpus.
`corpus/curation/project-glosses.tsv` records old and new gloss. A sample of 40 was read by hand: the glosses are
definitions of the word's use, 917 of the 937 are distinct (the others are true synonyms such as `anybody` and
`anyone`). No placeholder remains (`detect` reports 0).

The glosses live where the seeder and the compiler read them: `data/gloss` (937 rows of 73 files, all of `dema-syn-*`
synsets; no row of an Open English WordNet synset was touched, so OEWN glosses stay verbatim) and the `gloss` column of
`corpus/curation/senses.tsv` for the 135 curated project senses (the seeder writes the curation gloss, a second
`seed-inventory` run changes nothing). `data/gloss` changed because that is where the placeholder text was stored (the
generic text was written by the seeder into the data table, which is the committed source of record,
`data/schema.yaml`); the edits are limited to those rows. `docs/user_guide/appendix-senses.md` was regenerated by
`appendix`; `validate-data` and `compile-image` pass.

## Pool of real text

Only text that is in the public domain in the United States, or CC0, is used (the evidence of every document is in the
open English part-of-speech corpus project: terms pages with their hashes, the book-by-book publication years): 175
Project Gutenberg books published 1930 or earlier (header, footer and licence stripped), and the works of US federal
employees, which are in the public domain in the United States under 17 U.S.C. §105 and the policy of the agency: 379
VOA Learning English staff-written articles, 443 USGS, 363 NPS and 200 National Weather Service pages, and (from wave 2)
11 FAA publications; the Tatoeba sentences of the CC0 export are CC0 1.0. Excluded: Wikipedia, Wiktionary, any CC BY-SA
text, Universal Dependencies data, news sites, NOS, NASA, the FAA documents with third-party rights (see below) and the
Tatoeba CC BY sentences. The pool (`pool.py build`) holds **599,677 sentences** (465,525 Gutenberg, 66,556 FAA, 28,315
Tatoeba, 14,779 VOA, 11,257 NPS, 9,050 USGS, 4,195 NWS) of 6 to 34 words, deduplicated; the sentences that the corpus or
the suite already contains are never candidates. Every provenance row records the licence basis of its document in the
column `licence_basis` ("Public domain in the United States: ..." with the reason).

**FAA text** (`tools/quota-fill/faa.py`). Included, under 17 U.S.C. §105 (works of the US government, third-party
material excluded): the Aviation Maintenance Technician Handbooks (General FAA-H-8083-30B, Airframe FAA-H-8083-31B,
Powerplant FAA-H-8083-32B), the Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA-H-8083-25C), the Airplane (FAA-H-8083-3C),
Helicopter (FAA-H-8083-21B), Glider (FAA-H-8083-13B) and Balloon (FAA-H-8083-11B) Flying Handbooks, Plane Sense
(FAA-H-8083-19A) and Advisory Circulars 43.13-1B and 43.13-2B. Excluded entirely: the Parachute Rigger Handbook (third-party
copyright), the Aviation Weather Handbook (UCAR copyright), Aerodynamics for Naval Aviators and the MC-4 manual (they
restrict reproduction). Text only: the front matter is skipped, and every page that carries a credit marker ("courtesy
of", "used with permission", a copyright notice or sign, "reproduced", a photo credit) is dropped whole, so no passage
that is credited to a third party remains; figure captions are removed by the sentence filter. Each handbook is one
document in the pool (family `faa`, document id and URL in `sources.tsv`), so the document roles below apply to it as a
whole. The FAA text helps the technical senses (`arm`, `seal`, `bearing`, `bleed`, `torque`, `run`, `check`, `lead`,
`flush`, `tap`), which the other text rarely shows.

## Method

1. **Harvest.** For each lemma of the wave a sample of candidate sentences is drawn with a word that is a form of the
   lemma (round robin over the forms, 55% Gutenberg, 15% Tatoeba, 30% government text, at most 5 per document). The
   labeler (`gpt-6.1-sol`) sees the sentence, the target word and the full sense list (id, part of speech, gloss) of
   every lemma and part of speech the lexicon allows for the form, and answers one sense with a confidence or `unsure`
   (`tools/quota-fill/prompts/sentence-labeler.md`). Only high-confidence answers are kept.
2. **Selection.** For each sense with a deficit, round robin over forms and documents (at most 3 sentences of a sense
   and 10 of a lemma from one document), no sentence that is a near-duplicate (token Jaccard 0.8) of a corpus or suite
   sentence of the lemma. Held-out sentences are filled first with real text; the anchors take the rest.
3. **Top-up by writing.** `gpt-5.6-luna` writes 1.5 sentences per missing sentence (6 to 45 per sense) from the gloss,
   the dictionary examples, the allowed forms and the other senses of the lemma; `gpt-6.1-sol` labels them blind (the
   intended sense is not shown, the full sense list of the form is); a sentence is kept only when sol answers the
   intended sense with high confidence. Mechanical checks: one braced target, the form allows the part of speech of the
   sense, 6 to 30 words, ASCII, no duplicate, no near-duplicate.
4. **Choosers.** A chooser needs rows for every lemma of a form. The forms of the former report (the participles that
   are adjectives, `advanced`, `bloomed`) were harvested with a lemma-choice prompt (the labeler picks the lemma among
   all lemmas of the form, `prompts/lemma-chooser.md`); the sentences of the fine-sense rows of a form shared by several
   lemmas are chooser rows too; the forms that real text could not fill got written sentences that `gpt-6.1-sol`
   confirmed blind.

## Targeted rows

Every quota-filled row is **targeted**: found or written for one sense (or one form). Its provenance row
(`corpus/provenance/quota-*`) has the extra columns `selection = targeted`, `selection_reason` (`sense=<id>` or
`form=<form>`) and `found_by` (the harvest or the writer, and the labeler). The encoder corpus (`anchors`, `holdout`,
`choosers`) is a balanced classifier corpus: it must never be used for usage statistics (tagger counts, frequency
priors). Its held-out sentences are fine for classifier evaluation, but `evaluate` reports them by origin (targeted real
text, earlier harvest, LLM-written) and says that the figures are not representative; the representative measures are
the open part-of-speech corpus and SemCor.

## Real-text threshold calibration

The ablation of the anchors (training on all anchors beats real-only by 15.6 points and written-only by 8.5 points on
real text) found the real-text sense precision far below the 95% target: BERT 87.5% and MiniLM 85.7% on the real
held-out sentences, because the thresholds were chosen by cross-validation on the anchors, which are balanced and partly
written. The builder now moves the thresholds on real text (requirement `WordSense-WordSenseBuilder-RealCalibration`,
design in `docs/design/word-sense/word-sense-builder.md`):

- **Split by document.** The real held-out rows (provenance `origin = real`, 6,730 rows) are split by the first bit of
  the SHA-256 of `document|<document id>`: a document is a Gutenberg book, a VOA article or a government page (the part
  of the source key before `#`), a Tatoeba sentence is its own document. Result: **calibration** 3,317 rows, **test**
  3,413 rows; every sentence of a document is on one side; the written rows (7,738) are in neither. The rule is in
  `RealSplit.cs`; there is no seed.
- **Document-disjointness from the anchors.** The calibration and test documents are disjoint from each other. The 6,730
  real held-out rows come from 4,282 documents (3,754 are Tatoeba sentences), but 401 of the 528 other documents
  (Gutenberg, VOA and agency pages; 2,823 held-out rows) also supply 5,396 real anchors: the earlier harvests did not
  separate them. `evaluate` therefore also reports the test rows of documents that supply no anchor (1,944 rows). From
  wave 2 on the harvest gives every document one role by hash (a quarter of the documents are evaluation documents that
  supply held-out rows only, the others supply anchors only), so new rows are disjoint.
- **Calibration.** The final thresholds are the cross-validation ones, raised until the answers on the calibration rows
  reach the target: a lemma with 20 or more calibration rows (22 lemmas) gets a threshold of its own, never below its
  adjusted one; all other lemmas share one pooled shift; a level that was never answered stays never answered; the group
  thresholds move only when at least 30 calibration group answers can verify them (otherwise they are kept and the run
  says so). The lemma-level target is 0.97, two points above the 95% aimed at on the pipeline, because the precision of
  the whole pipeline (the analysis and the form chooser) on the same sentences is about two points below the lemma-level
  precision (measured: calibrated lemma-level 95.1% against 92.6% pipeline at the target 0.95 on MiniLM). The sweep of
  the target on the real test split (sense answers, share and precision): MiniLM 0.95: 16.7% at 92.5%, 0.97: 7.9% at
  94.1%, 0.98: 5.2% at 93.8%; BERT 0.95: 37.5% at 93.4%, 0.97: 16.3% at 96.6%. MiniLM saturates near 94%: about 7% of
  the gold labels are wrong (the ablation's blind judge), which caps the measurable precision of a level.

## Wave 1 in detail

- **Harvest**: 48 lemmas with a deficit, 18,010 candidates in 79 batches of `gpt-6.1-sol`: 15,824 labeled (87.9%; 14,868
  high, 956 low), 2,186 `unsure` (12.1%). Selected: 3,361 anchors and 1,400 held-out sentences (Gutenberg 2,353, VOA
  696, Tatoeba CC0 549, USGS 483, NPS 445, NWS 235).
- **Written**: round 1 asked for 9,758 sentences for 563 senses in 47 batches of `gpt-5.6-luna`; 9,564 passed the checks
  (85 non-ASCII, 106 form not allowed, 3 other); in 42 blind batches of sol 7,835 (81.9%) got the intended sense (7,437
  with high confidence), 1,188 another sense, 541 `unsure`. Selected 4,226 anchors and 677 held-out sentences. Round 2
  for the 94 senses still short: 1,292 asked, 1,254 valid, only 418 (33%) with the intended sense (348 high); selected
  262 + 22. Those senses are the hard ones: the blind labeler prefers a sibling sense.
- **Choosers**: 266 forms, 8,299 candidates in 37 batches, 7,990 high-confidence lemmas; 3,035 + 959 rows. Then 72
  (form, lemma) pairs were written (576 sentences in 3 luna batches, 502 confirmed by sol in 3 batches): 374 + 128 rows.
  Sentences of fine-sense rows of shared forms: 867 + 340 (real), 618 + 98 (written), 48 (round 2).
- **Batches**: `gpt-6.1-sol` 79 + 42 + 6 + 37 + 3 = 167 (plus the batches of the gloss writing), `gpt-5.6-luna` 47 + 7 +
  3 = 57.
- **Not filled**: 47 short and 10 empty senses of the 961 senses of the wave 1 lemmas (373 anchors and 44 held-out
  sentences missing); `corpus/coverage/quota-unfilled.tsv` lists them with the number of written sentences and how many
  the blind labeler confirmed (for example `call#decide`: 25 written, none confirmed). They cannot be filled credibly
  with this method (the sense is a near-duplicate of a sibling, or the gloss is too thin to write to); they are
  candidates for the group and curation review.

## Wave 2 in detail

Lemmas of ranks 51-120 (by WordNet 3.0 tag count) with a deficit, plus the senses that need extra **real** anchors.

- **Extra real anchors** (`confusion.py`): from the answers of both encoders on the real *calibration* rows only (never
  the test split), every sense that took part in a fine top-1 error (as the gold or as the predicted sense) gets a target
  of 8 real anchors (5 when it took part in one error only), and a sense of a lemma of waves 1-2 with fewer than 3 real
  anchors gets 3 (the rare senses); 1,893 senses are targeted (655 confused, 5,709 real anchors wanted). Real text filled
  1,316 of the wanted anchors in wave 2; 4,393 for 1,332 senses remain for the later waves (the pool rarely has a
  sentence of a rare sense).
- **Document roles**: a document that supplies anchors never supplies held-out rows and the other way round. A new
  document gets one role by the hash of its id: a quarter of the documents are evaluation documents (held-out rows
  only), the others supply anchors only, so the new held-out rows are disjoint from the anchors by document. Most
  Gutenberg books already supply both and were therefore little used (146 rows); the sentences came from Tatoeba CC0
  (1,217), the FAA (582), VOA (581), USGS (504), NPS (468) and NWS (268).
- **Harvest**: 70 wave lemmas and 50 earlier lemmas with extras, 12,259 candidates in 54 batches of `gpt-6.1-sol`, and
  1,783 FAA candidates for 47 technical lemmas (arm, seal, mount, bearing, bleed, torque, run, check, lead, flush, tap,
  ...) in 8 more batches: 14,042 candidates, 12,308 labeled (87.7%; 11,399 high, 909 low), 1,734 `unsure`. Selected:
  3,407 anchors and 359 held-out sentences (anchors: FAA 555, Tatoeba 1,016, VOA 550, NPS 440, NWS 235, USGS 469,
  Gutenberg 142; held-out: Tatoeba 201, NWS 33, USGS 35, VOA 31, NPS 28, FAA 27, Gutenberg 4). The FAA handbooks of the
  evaluation role are Plane Sense and AC 43.13-2B; the others supply anchors.
- **Written**: 17,179 sentences asked for 963 senses in 82 batches of `gpt-5.6-luna`; 16,972 passed the checks (178
  non-ASCII, 15 form not allowed, 13 braces, 1 near-duplicate); in 74 blind batches of sol 13,852 (81.6%) got the intended
  sense (13,036 with high confidence), 1,740 another sense, 1,380 `unsure`. Selected **all** verified written anchors
  (no cap): 6,589 anchors and 2,042 held-out sentences (flagged as written by their provenance `origin`).
- **Choosers**: sentences of the fine-sense rows of shared forms: 504 + 31 (real), 1,662 + 546 (written); 39 forms
  that still lacked a lemma, 1,260 candidates in 6 batches (1,001 high, 252 `unsure`, 7 dropped): 428 + 138 rows. Forms
  with chooser rows now: 399, of which 355 are complete (31 forms lack a lemma, most of them proper nouns or
  abbreviations such as `LED`, `State` and `SEAL`, or a form whose second lemma has no real sentence). MiniLM form
  choosers on 3,041 held-out chooser sentences: top-1 91.02% (answered 76.72% at precision 96.23%).
- **Batches**: `gpt-6.1-sol` 62 + 74 + 6 = 142, `gpt-5.6-luna` 82.
- **Retrain**: 768 lemmas per encoder (MiniLM: fine threshold for 690, never for 78; 61 lemmas with one anchored sense
  have no weights). Half-precision gate: MiniLM 76.46% on 16,721 held-out rows, BERT 84.74%.
- **Suite** (348 rows): fine MiniLM 92.0% (H 91.7, M 96.0, C 87.0; 91.7% before), BERT base cased 94.8% (H 94.7, M
  96.8, C 92.4; unchanged); part of speech of the selected analysis: Basic 91.7%, MiniLM 94.5%, BERT 97.4%.
- **Not filled**: after waves 1-2, 153 short and 30 empty senses of the 2,023 senses of the 120 lemmas (1,128 anchors
  and 150 held-out sentences missing; `corpus/coverage/quota-unfilled.tsv` lists 183 senses). Over all senses 2,673 meet
  the quota, 2,507 are short and 1,676 empty (28,221 anchors and 7,731 held-out sentences missing).
